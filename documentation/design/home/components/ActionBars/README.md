Two page-level bars: the selection bar offers actions for selected objects, the confirmation bar asks to keep a risky change before a timer undoes it.

- Selection bar: appears when at least one object is selected; says how many, lists only the actions valid for this selection, ends with "Clear selection". Computer: sticks to the bottom of the content. Phone: sits directly above the taskbar and shows at most two actions plus "More".
- Confirmation bar: used for network changes. Computer: above the content, under the page header. Phone: pinned above the taskbar. The keeping action is `primary`; the countdown is in the bold line.
- Only one such bar at a time; the confirmation bar wins.
